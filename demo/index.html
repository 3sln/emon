The HTML is shown in a sandboxed frame without scripts; its figures are inline images. The JSON is emon's Model, the same on every platform emon runs on.

Good to know

  • It loads what the document needs. A PDF with a healthy text layer never loads pdfium (5 MiB of WebAssembly, for drawing pages) or Tesseract (10 MiB, for reading pictures of text).
  • Fonts. pdfium draws a font that a PDF names but does not embed from its own built-in set, not from your system's fonts, so the lettering inside a figure may differ a little from a desktop viewer's. The text of the HTML is the PDF's own and does not depend on it.
  • Browsers. emon's module needs WebAssembly's standardised exception handling: Chrome 137, Firefox 131, Safari 18.4 or newer. Large pages are limited to 64 megapixels when drawn.
  • Free software. emon is MIT-licensed; pdfium, Tesseract and the other components this page ships are listed, with their licences, in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md. build.json has the versions and checksums of this build.
